About Hamro Hospital

Hamro Hospital is a leading healthcare institution in Nepal, dedicated to providing accessible, high-quality medical services to all communities. Our mission is to ensure every patient receives compassionate, evidence-based care in a modern, safe environment.

Our Mission

To deliver comprehensive healthcare services that meet international standards while remaining accessible and affordable for all Nepali communities.

Our Vision

To become Nepal's most trusted hospital network, combining technology-enabled care with traditional medical expertise to serve every corner of the country.
